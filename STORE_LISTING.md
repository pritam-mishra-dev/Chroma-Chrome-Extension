# Chrome Web Store listing

**Short description (≤132):**
Your YouTube, painted by the video. Accents, progress bar and a soft glow shift with every scene — smooth, light, and private.

**Long description:**
Chroma recolors YouTube in real time using the colors of whatever you're watching. As scenes change, accents, links, the Subscribe button, the progress bar, the scrollbar and a soft glow around the player cross-fade to match.

• Live, Static (one palette from the thumbnail) or Custom color modes
• Every color is contrast-checked against YouTube's dark or light theme
• Works on watch pages, theater mode, Shorts and the miniplayer; ads freeze the palette
• Performance modes for laptops; respects Reduce Motion and Windows High Contrast
• Toggle instantly with Alt+Shift+C (Ctrl+Shift+C on Mac)

**Privacy:** No data leaves your device. Chroma reads a tiny 64×36 downscaled frame locally to pick colors. It only contacts i.ytimg.com to fetch a video's public thumbnail when frames can't be read. No analytics, no accounts, no tracking.
